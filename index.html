So linha

The private publishing tool of the YouTube channel “So…”.

So linha is a small program that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads the channel's finished videos to YouTube, sets their title, description and thumbnail, schedules when they go public, and reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, reach) so the owner can see how each video is doing.

It is used only by the owner of the channel, on his own channel. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

Privacy policy

Em português: o So linha é a ferramenta particular de publicação do canal “So…”. Roda no computador do dono do canal, envia os vídeos prontos, ajusta título, descrição e capa, agenda a publicação e lê as estatísticas do próprio canal. Só o dono usa, só no próprio canal. Política de privacidade.
